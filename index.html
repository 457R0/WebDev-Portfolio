About

The toolbox

I run IT by T, a freelance web development and IT support practice based in Alexandria, MN. I build sites and small tools for local businesses, and full applications when a project calls for one — from front-end interfaces down to the backend and data layer underneath.

Before development, I spent time in retail supervision and pharmacy tech work — jobs that taught me to move fast, stay accurate under pressure, and communicate clearly with people who aren't technical. That shows up in how I scope and deliver client projects now: plain language, realistic timelines, working software.

Self-taught and self-directed — I learn by building, and I keep a running notebook of what I pick up along the way.
